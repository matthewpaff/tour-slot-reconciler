# History and provenance

This original learning project was created in November 2026 with AI assistance.
Fixture author dates and all committer dates follow a simulated 2023 calendar.
They do not establish past work, employment, or repository age. Implementation
and documentation author dates retain their actual 2026 values.

## Calendar and career model

The source blueprint is explicitly hypothetical. This is an independent behavioral
prototype, not employer-owned software or a claim of period-compatible production code.
Blueprint section: Z01.
The combined 2020–2025 schedule excludes U.S. federal legal and observed holidays.
This year leaves 63 of 105 weekend dates empty; weekday gaps and daily volumes vary.
Existing projects included in the plan are counted once in the yearly totals.

This repository has 547 generated validation cases. They are data variations,
not independent features. No upstream histories or author identities were copied.
The November 2026 calendar revision preserved every code, test, example, and fixture
blob and every commit subject. Fixture author dates were rescheduled; author identities
were preserved. Earlier date notes describe prior modeled schedules.

Independent mirrors, portable bundles, and full old-to-new ID maps preserve the
pre-revision history. Repository creation and server-side activity dates are unchanged.
Shared branch snapshots do not multiply contribution credit.
